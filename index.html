Blue 2026 · IISc

Learning What to Remember

A vision-language model watching a long video has limited memory and does not know the question yet. Which frames should it keep? I reproduced the SimpleStream baseline (64.49 against the paper's 65.13), then trained a memory that scores frames by future answerability. It did not beat keeping the four most recent frames, and the write-up says so.
